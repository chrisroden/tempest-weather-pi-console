## Tour 4: Theme Variants

Image files:

- `Wiki/images/dashboard-theme-default.png`
- `Wiki/images/dashboard-theme-walnut.png`
- `Wiki/images/dashboard-theme-snow.png`
- `Wiki/images/dashboard-theme-white-ash.png`

![Theme Default](images/dashboard-theme-default.png)
![Theme Walnut](images/dashboard-theme-walnut.png)
![Theme Snow](images/dashboard-theme-snow.png)
![Theme White Ash](images/dashboard-theme-white-ash.png)

Callouts:

1. Same layout, different visual theme
2. Verify text contrast in day/night lighting
3. Pick the theme that is easiest to read at distance

Theme detail:

- `Snow` and `White-Ash` differ primarily in the darker grey shades.
- `White-Ash` exists for screens where those greys were hard to see in `Snow`.
